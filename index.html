中文

HanlongGame（下称"我们"）是《形界塔防》（英文名 Shapehold，下称"本游戏"）的开发者与发行者。本政策说明我们如何处理你的信息。

一、我们不收集个人数据

本游戏在运行过程中不会向我们的服务器或任何第三方服务器发送、上传或共享你的个人数据。游戏内没有账号注册、没有聊天、没有排行榜、没有内购与广告，也没有任何形式的数据统计或分析 SDK。

二、游戏进度保存在你自己的设备上

你的存档、设置与历史记录只写入本机的游戏用户数据目录（Windows 下为 %APPDATA% 内本游戏专属的存档目录）。我们不会读取或存储其中的内容。卸载游戏时你可以自行选择是否保留该目录。

三、Steam 平台相关数据

你在 Steam 上的账号信息、购买记录、下载记录与社区内容等由 Valve 收集和管理，受《Steam 订户协议》与 Valve 隐私政策约束： https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/。我们不会获取上述数据。

四、Steam 云存档（如启用）

若本游戏启用了 Steam 云存档，你的存档文件将由 Valve 通过 Steam 云在你的设备之间同步，相关存储与处理由 Valve 负责，适用上一条中的 Valve 政策。我们不接触这些文件。

五、未成年人

本游戏不收集任何人的个人数据，包括未成年人。

六、政策变更

如果本游戏的隐私做法发生变化（例如未来加入联网功能），我们会更新本页面并修改上方的"最后更新"日期。

七、联系我们

任何疑问或请求，请联系：HLGServices@protonmail.com

English

HanlongGame ("we") is the developer and publisher of Shapehold (《形界塔防》, the "Game"). This policy explains how we handle information.

1. We do not collect personal data

The Game does not send, upload or share any personal data to us or to any third party while it runs. There is no account sign-in, no chat, no leaderboard, no in-app purchases, no advertising, and no analytics or telemetry SDK.

2. Your progress stays on your own device

Save files, settings and records are written only to the game's user data folder on your machine (on Windows typically %APPDATA% — in a folder dedicated to this game). We never read or store the contents of that folder.

3. Steam platform data

Your Steam account, purchase history, download records and community content are collected and managed by Valve and governed by the Steam Subscriber Agreement and Valve's Privacy Policy: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/. We do not receive any of that data.

4. Steam Cloud (if enabled)

If Steam Cloud saves are enabled for this Game, your save files are synced between your devices by Valve through Steam Cloud. That storage and processing is Valve's responsibility under the terms above; we do not access those files.

5. Children

The Game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If our privacy practices change (for example if we add online features), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact

Questions or requests: HLGServices@protonmail.com
